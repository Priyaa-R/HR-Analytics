# 👩‍💼 HR Analytics Project | Excel Dashboard
This project focuses on analyzing HR data to derive insights related to workforce demographics, attrition trends, departmental performance, and employee satisfaction. The goal was to support HR decision-making through a dynamic Excel dashboard.

## Situation
An organization was experiencing challenges in understanding patterns in employee turnover, departmental efficiency, and satisfaction levels. The HR team needed a clearer picture of workforce trends to improve retention and optimize human capital management.

## Task
Design a solution using only Excel that:
     - Cleans and analyzes raw HR data
     - Calculates HR-related KPIs
     - Visualizes attrition patterns, department trends, and workforce demographics
     - Helps HR make data-driven decisions

## Action
- Cleaned and structured the raw HR dataset in Excel
- Used formulas and pivot tables to extract key metrics
- Built interactive charts and slicers to allow dynamic filtering
- Designed a professional dashboard layout focusing on clarity and insight delivery

## Result
- Delivered an interactive, user-friendly HR dashboard
- Helped identify departments with high attrition
- Uncovered satisfaction trends and performance gaps
- Enabled HR to take proactive measures for talent retention and engagement

## KPIs Calculated
- Employee Count
- Attrition Rate
- Average Age
- Average Monthly Income
- Job Satisfaction Level
- Active vs Exited Employees

## Chart Trends
- Gender Distribution
- Department-wise Attrition
- Attrition by Job Role
- Monthly Income by Job Level
- Education Field vs Attrition
- Performance Rating vs Satisfaction
- Age Distribution
- Years at Company vs Attrition

## Insights Derived
- High Attrition: Specific departments showed higher attrition rates
- Income Gap: Differences in salary distribution by job level and education
- Demographics: Gender and age distribution impact on attrition
- Job Satisfaction: Correlated with performance ratings and income
- Education Field: Certain educational backgrounds had higher exit trends

## Tools and technologied Used
Microsoft Excel 
Pivot Tables & Charts
Slicers and Timelines
Conditional Formatting
Formulas (IF, AVERAGEIFS, COUNTIFS, etc.)
